Buying meat by the geun

At a butcher or a Korean BBQ restaurant that sells meat by weight, prices are often shown per geun (600 g) or per 100 g. One geun of pork belly (삼겹살 한 근) feeds about 2–3 people. Since 2007 shops are supposed to label prices in metric units, so you'll usually see grams on the label even when people say “geun”.

Gold by the don

Gold prices in Korea are quoted per don (3.75 g). A traditional gift for a baby's first birthday (돌) is a gold ring of one don (한 돈 금반지). To compare with international prices quoted per troy ounce (31.1 g), one troy ounce is about 8.3 don.

Rice and beans by the doe

At traditional markets, grains are measured by volume in a square wooden box called a doe. Because it's volume, the weight depends on what's inside. A doe of rice weighs roughly 1.6 kg.
