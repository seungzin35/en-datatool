Three ways to count age in Korea

NameHow it's countedWhere it's used
International age
만 나이		Starts at 0 and goes up on your birthday, like most countries.The official standard for laws, contracts and government services since June 28, 2023.
Korean age
세는 나이		You are 1 at birth and everyone gets a year older on January 1.Traditional, still heard in casual conversation, especially among older people.
Year age
연 나이		Current year minus birth year. Everyone born in the same year has the same age all year.Some laws still use it, such as the youth protection rules for buying alcohol and tobacco, and military service.

What changed in 2023?

On June 28, 2023, Korea made international age the standard for legal and administrative purposes. Before that, many people gave their “Korean age” in daily life, which is one or two years older than international age. You will still hear Korean age sometimes, and some rules that are written in year age kept it.

Drinking and smoking age in Korea

You can legally buy alcohol and tobacco from January 1 of the year you turn 19 in international age, because the youth protection rules use year age. For example, anyone born in 2008 can buy alcohol from January 1, 2027. Shops and bars may ask for ID.

Your zodiac animal (띠)

Koreans often ask “what animal are you?” (무슨 띠예요?) as a polite way to ask your birth year. The animals follow a 12-year cycle: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. Strictly the year changes at Lunar New Year, so if you were born in January or early February you may belong to the previous animal; in everyday conversation most people simply go by the calendar year.
