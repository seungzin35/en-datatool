Long weekends in 2027

  • Seollal (Lunar New Year): Sat Feb 6 – Tue Feb 9, 4 days off, because Seollal falls on a Sunday.
  • Labor Day: Sat May 1 – Mon May 3. Take Tue May 4 off and you get five days through Children's Day on Wed May 5.
  • Constitution Day: Sat Jul 17 – Mon Jul 19.
  • Liberation Day: Sun Aug 15 – Mon Aug 16.
  • Chuseok: Tue Sep 14 – Thu Sep 16. Take Mon Sep 13 and Fri Sep 17 off for a 9-day break.
  • October: Sun Oct 3 – Mon Oct 4 (National Foundation Day) and Sat Oct 9 – Mon Oct 11 (Hangul Day).
  • Christmas: Sat Dec 25 – Mon Dec 27.

What is a substitute holiday?

When certain holidays fall on a weekend, or overlap with another holiday, the next working day becomes a substitute holiday (대체공휴일). In 2027 there are seven, which is why many holidays turn into three-day weekends.

Do private companies have to give these days off?

Since 2022, workplaces with 5 or more employees must give public holidays as paid days off (or pay holiday-work rates). Small workplaces with fewer than 5 employees are not required to, so check your contract. Labor Day (May 1) has long been a paid day off for employees under labor law.

Travel tips for Seollal and Chuseok

  • Book trains early. KTX and SRT tickets for the holiday days sell out within minutes when sales open, usually a few weeks before.
  • Expect closures. Many restaurants, small shops and markets close on the main day. Large supermarkets may close for a day or two.
  • Seoul gets quiet. Many residents travel to their hometowns, so the city itself is calmer, and palaces and museums often stay open.
  • Banks and government offices close on all public holidays. ATMs and convenience stores stay open.
