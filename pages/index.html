Why these tools?

Korean prices, salaries and apartment listings use units that many newcomers have never seen. A rent deposit of “1억 2천만 원”, a “34평” apartment or a payslip full of insurance deductions are hard to read at first. These tools give you the answer quickly and explain how it is calculated, so you can check the numbers yourself.
