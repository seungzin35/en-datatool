Korean size chart: clothes and shoes

Korean shops use millimeters for shoes and numbers like 95, 100 or 55 for clothes. Convert them to the sizes you know.

Korean shoe sizes are your foot length in millimeters, in steps of 5 mm. Not sure? Measure your foot from heel to longest toe and round up to the next 5 mm.

Women's clothing sizes

Women's clothes in Korea use two systems. Older and department-store labels use 44 / 55 / 66 / 77 / 88; newer labels use the bust size in cm, 85 / 90 / 95 / 100 / 105.

Pants and jeans

Pants are usually sized by waist in inches (28, 30, 32…), the same as in the US, or sometimes in cm (e.g. 76 = about 30 in). To convert: inches × 2.54 = cm. Free-size (프리사이즈, “FREE”) items usually fit Korean sizes 55–66 for women or 95–100 for men.

Shopping tips

  • Online shops list the garment's real measurements (실측 사이즈) in cm: chest width (가슴단면), length (총장), sleeve (소매길이). Measure a shirt you own and compare. Note that “chest width” is half the chest, measured flat.
  • Exchanges and returns (교환·반품) on Korean shopping sites often cost a shipping fee of a few thousand won, so it pays to check measurements first.
  • Shoe brands differ by up to half a size. If a store lets you, try both your usual size and 5 mm up.

Sizes are typical values from common brand charts and can vary by brand and cut.
