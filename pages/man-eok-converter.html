How Korean numbers work

English groups digits in threes (thousand, million, billion). Korean groups them in fours. The key units are 만 (man, 10,000), 억 (eok, 100,000,000) and 조 (jo, 1,000,000,000,000). Smaller words 천 (cheon, 1,000), 백 (baek, 100) and 십 (sip, 10) are used inside each group.

So 3억 5천만 원 means 3 × 100,000,000 + 5,000 × 10,000 = 350,000,000 won, or 350 million won.

Where you will see these numbers

  • Rent: a listing like 보증금 1000 / 월세 50 usually means a 10,000,000 won deposit and 500,000 won monthly rent. Real-estate sites often leave out 만, so the numbers are in units of 10,000 won.
  • Jeonse: lump-sum deposits are usually written in 억, such as 2억 5천 (250,000,000 won).
  • Salaries: annual pay is often said as 연봉 3천 or 3,500만 원 (35,000,000 won a year). Try the take-home pay calculator to see the monthly amount.

About the exchange rate
