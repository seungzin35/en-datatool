Korean address in English

Paste a Korean address in Hangul. You'll get it in English order and spelling, ready for an overseas order form, a visa application or a parcel.

English address
—

Building and apartment names are spelled out letter by letter. If the building has its own English name (for example on its website), use that instead. Notes in brackets, such as (역삼동), are left out.

Always double-check with the official English address on juso.go.kr (Korea Address Information). Korean mail is sorted by the 5-digit postal code, so include it whenever you can.

How Korean addresses are written in English

Korean addresses go from the biggest area to the smallest. In English the order is reversed, from the smallest to the biggest, just like a Western address.

Road-name vs. lot-number addresses

Since 2014 Korea's official addresses use road names (도로명주소), such as Sejong-daero 209. You will still see the older lot-number style (지번주소), such as Yeoksam-dong 737, on old documents and in everyday speech. This tool handles both, but the road-name address is the one to use on forms.

Tips for online shopping and forms

  • Address line 1: building number and road, e.g. “16 Sillim-ro 23-gil”.
  • Address line 2: apartment and unit, e.g. “101-dong 202-ho”.
  • City: the district and city, e.g. “Gwanak-gu, Seoul”. State/Province: the province or metropolitan city.
  • Delivery inside Korea: couriers work best with the Korean address. Give the English one only when a site doesn't accept Hangul.

Romanization follows the Revised Romanization of Korean, with hyphens before address units as in the official English addresses. Some well-known names keep traditional spellings (for example Teheran-ro).
